Baked with love since day one

Crème Mori started in a tiny home kitchen with one oven and a cookie recipe that friends kept asking for. Today we still mix, scoop and bake by hand, every single morning.

We use real butter, local flour and chocolate we’d happily eat on its own. No shortcuts, no preservatives, just good things baked slowly.
